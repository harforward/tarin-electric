$12.69/job — What It Means
Labor-Only Model
$8,425 total supplies over 664 invoices = $12.69 per job. This is the single strongest data signal. It means customers supply major materials — the owner charges for labor only. This is common in commercial facility work, sub-contracted labor, and property management accounts.

Until bank statements confirm otherwise, the labor-only model is the correct one to use.
Bank statements will answer this permanently. One session of reconciliation = accurate books forever.
