Key Insight on Job Supplies
$12.69
Average materials cost per invoice (QB confirmed)
This is the strongest signal in the data. $12.69/job in recorded supplies over 664 invoices strongly indicates customers are supplying major materials, or the business is a pure labor model. This is actually common in:

• Commercial facility work — facilities team orders parts, electrician installs
• Warranty/repair work — manufacturer-supplied parts
• Sub-contracted labor — GC supplies everything, electrician just bills labor
• Property management — management company has standing supply accounts
One key question to confirm: does the owner use a personal credit card or cash for materials that never hits QB? Bank statements will answer this definitively.
