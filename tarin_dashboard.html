What Each Phase Actually Requires — Honest Checklist
Phase 1 → $1M (Now — End 2027)
  • ✅RevenueAlready at $1.05M run rate. Just sustain.
  • ⚡CommercialLock in 2–3 facility management contracts. Malcolm Drilling, Eaton, A-Prime are the template.
  • 📋QB / BooksGet bank statements. Close the expense gap. Accurate books = ability to get credit.
  • 📱Marketing$500/mo Google Ads or Yelp. Adds ~$25K/mo revenue at current close rates.
  • 🤝Subs1–2 trusted subcontractors on call. Don't turn down work — sub it out.
  • 📞RetentionCall top 10 churned clients. Offer a maintenance check-in. Re-win 2–3.
  • 💳CreditOpen a business credit card for materials. Separates cash flow from personal.
Phase 2 → $2M (2028)
  • 👷HiringHire 2–3 licensed journeymen. W-2 or 1099. Each adds ~$180K revenue.
  • 🔧SystemsScheduling software (Jobber or ServiceTitan Lite — ~$100/mo). Stop losing jobs to disorganization.
  • 📄EstimatingStandardize job estimates. Template pricing sheet. Stop leaving money on the table.
  • 🏦BankingBusiness line of credit ($50–100K). Needed for large material purchases on commercial jobs.
  • 🛡️InsuranceWorkers comp will jump to $10–20K/yr with employees. Budget for it.
  • 🚐Vehicles1–2 work vans. Can lease. Crews need independent transportation.
  • 📊BookkeeperMonthly bookkeeper ($300–500/mo). You can't scale and do your own books.
